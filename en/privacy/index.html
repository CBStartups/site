1. Who we are

Companhia Brasileira de Startups (“CBStartups”, “we”), registered under CNPJ no. 23.557.073/0001-84, is a Brazilian venture builder that creates and operates digital products. For the purposes of Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law no. 13,709/2018 — LGPD), we are the controller of the personal data processed in our products, unless another company is expressly named as controller for a specific service.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to anyone who visits our websites, creates an account or uses our platforms, apps (including those distributed through Google Play and integrated with Google services), APIs and support channels (“Services”).

Some Services may have supplementary privacy notices with their own details. In those cases, the supplementary notice and this policy apply together; in case of conflict, whichever is more protective of you prevails.

When a business customer uses our Services to process third-party data (for example, its own customers' data), that customer is the controller of such data and we act as processor, following its instructions, under article 39 of the LGPD.

4. How we use data

  • To create and maintain your account, authenticate access and provide the Services you requested;
  • To perform contracts, issue invoices and tax documents;
  • To provide support and answer requests;
  • To send operational communications, such as security notices, changes to the Services and confirmations;
  • To send marketing communications when you consent or when there is legitimate interest, always with an option to unsubscribe;
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • To measure usage and improve the Services, preferably with aggregated or anonymized data;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations and exercise rights in judicial, administrative or arbitration proceedings.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Legal bases

Each processing activity relies on one of the bases in article 7 of the LGPD:

  • Performance of a contract (art. 7, V): registration, provision of the Services, billing and support;
  • Compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation (art. 7, II): retention of access logs, tax and accounting obligations;
  • Regular exercise of rights (art. 7, VI): defense in proceedings;
  • Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX, and art. 10): security, fraud prevention, improvement of the Services and communications related to what you already use, always respecting your rights and expectations;
  • Consent (art. 7, I): access to data from connected accounts such as Google, device location, non-essential cookies and marketing when required. You may withdraw consent at any time (art. 8, § 5).

6. Google user data

Some Services let you sign in with your Google account or connect Google services. In those cases:

  • What we access: only the data in the scopes shown on Google's consent screen at the time of authorization, such as name, email and profile picture, and other data only when needed for the feature you requested;
  • How we use it: solely to authenticate you and to provide or improve user-facing features within the Service that requested access;
  • Sharing: we do not sell or transfer this data to third parties, except as necessary to provide or improve those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you;
  • Advertising: we do not use this data to serve ads, including personalized or retargeted ads;
  • Human reading: no one on our team reads this data, except with your express permission, for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), to comply with the law, or when aggregated and anonymized for internal operations;
  • Machine learning models: we do not use this data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models;
  • Storage and deletion: we store this data with the measures described under Security and only for as long as the feature requires. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and request deletion of the data at privacidade@cbstartups.com.br.

Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Sharing

We share personal data only when necessary and with the following parties:

  • Vendors acting as processors: cloud hosting and infrastructure, storage, email and messaging delivery, support, usage analytics and payment providers. They may use the data only to provide the contracted service to us;
  • Providers you connect: such as Google, according to the permissions you grant;
  • Group companies and successors: in case of corporate reorganization, merger, acquisition or sale of assets, keeping the protections of this policy;
  • Authorities: when there is a legal obligation or court order. Access logs are only provided under a court order, according to articles 10 and 22 of Brazil's Internet Civil Framework (Law no. 12,965/2014).

8. International transfers

Some infrastructure and cloud vendors may store or process data outside Brazil. Such transfers only take place under the cases in article 33 of the LGPD, such as standard contractual clauses, countries with an adequate level of protection, or when necessary to perform the contract with you, and with vendors that adopt security measures consistent with this policy.

9. How long we keep data

We keep data only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, and delete or anonymize it when processing ends (LGPD, arts. 15 and 16). As a rule:

  • Account data and content: while the account is active. After closure or a deletion request, they are deleted within a reasonable period, except in the cases below;
  • Application access logs: for 6 (six) months, in a controlled and secure environment, as required by article 15 of the Internet Civil Framework, or longer when requested by an authority;
  • Tax, accounting and contract data: for the periods required by applicable law;
  • Data needed to defend rights: for as long as legal claims may be brought or answered.

Retention in these cases is authorized by article 16 of the LGPD (compliance with a legal obligation and regular exercise of rights).

10. Security

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or improper disclosure (LGPD, art. 46), including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and hashed password storage;
  • need-to-know access control, with individual credentials and strong authentication for staff;
  • logging of access to internal systems and incident monitoring;
  • backups and infrastructure vendors with recognized security standards.

No system is completely immune to failures. If a security incident may cause relevant risk or harm, we will notify Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and the affected data subjects, under article 48 of the LGPD.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We use:

  • Essential cookies: keep the session, security and basic preferences. Without them, the Services do not work;
  • Performance and analytics cookies: measure usage to improve the Services, where present;
  • Third-party resources: embedded resources, such as the fonts served by Google Fonts on this site, which receive your IP address to deliver the content.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working.

12. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to obtain from us, at any time and upon request (LGPD, art. 18):

  • confirmation that processing takes place;
  • access to the data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in breach of the LGPD;
  • portability of the data to another provider, subject to ANPD regulations;
  • deletion of data processed based on your consent, except in the retention cases of article 16;
  • information about the public and private entities with which we share data;
  • information about the possibility of not giving consent and the consequences of refusal;
  • withdrawal of consent.

You may also object to processing based on another legal basis in case of non-compliance with the LGPD (art. 18, § 2), and request review of decisions made solely on automated processing that affect your interests (art. 20). If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may file a petition with the ANPD (art. 18, § 1).

13. How to exercise your rights

Send your request to privacidade@cbstartups.com.br, stating the Service you use and the right you wish to exercise. This includes requests to delete your account and associated data. To protect you, we may ask for information confirming your identity before acting on the request.

Requests are free of charge. Requests for confirmation of processing or full access to data are answered within 15 (fifteen) days from the request (LGPD, art. 19, II). If we cannot act on a request immediately, we will tell you why (art. 18, § 4).

14. Children and adolescents

Our Services are intended for people aged 18 or over or for legal entities, unless a specific Service states otherwise. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Where a Service admits this audience, processing will follow article 14 of the LGPD, always in the best interest of the child or adolescent and, for children, with the specific consent of at least one parent or legal guardian. If we identify children's data processed without such consent, it will be deleted.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Services or in the law. The last updated date appears at the top of the page. Material changes will be communicated through the Services or by email before they take effect and, where processing depends on consent, we will ask for new consent.

16. Contact and DPO

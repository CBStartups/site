About us

What is a venture builder?

A venture builder creates startups from scratch, with its own team, capital and infrastructure. Instead of betting on other people’s ideas, Companhia Brasileira de Startups builds, tests and operates every project in-house.

Since 2015 we have followed the same method: minimum product, fast validation and data-driven decisions. What works gets traction. What doesn’t is shut down, and the learning feeds the next project.

How it works

  1. 01

    Build

    We start from real problems in the Brazilian market and build a minimum product with our in-house team.

  2. 02

    Validate

    We put the product in front of real customers and measure usage, retention and willingness to pay.

  3. 03

    Scale

    Projects with market signal get investment, people and distribution to grow.

  4. 04

    Shut down

    What finds no room is closed fast. The learning stays for the next project.
